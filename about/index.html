About NeuroStat

A biopharmaceutical company based in Zürich, founded in 2003.

Who we are

NeuroStat develops treatments and diagnostic tools for the nervous system. We started with sleep and later added memory and the way people perform under strain. Some of our studies run with partners whose staff work under unusual conditions.

The company is listed on the SIX Swiss Exchange (NEUS). Headquarters are in Zürich, and there are offices in Basel, Boston and London.

Our history

  • 2003NeuroStat is founded in Zürich by Dr. Adriond Fisher together with a group of clinicians.
  • 2006Vesperil (ormelteon) is approved, the first NeuroStat product.
  • 2008Clinical research site opens in Basel.
  • 2010NeuroStat lists on the SIX Swiss Exchange under the ticker NEUS.
  • 2014Office opens in Boston for regulatory and clinical collaboration in North America.
  • 2015NeuroStat Baseline, a digital cognitive assessment platform, is launched.
  • 2017Lunavel (corexant) is approved for insomnia.
  • 2018Office opens in London.
  • 2019NeuroStat Halo, a dry-electrode EEG headband, receives CE marking.
  • 2020Migrestil (tolugepant) is approved for the acute treatment of migraine.
  • 2026A Phase III study of tolugepant in migraine prevention does not meet its primary endpoint.
  • 2028Fathom, a whole-body imaging system in which the patient floats in a tank of breathable fluid, receives CE marking.
  • 2028BOREAS-2, a Phase II study of NS-760 in extended offshore rotations, begins recruitment.

Responsibility

Our studies follow good clinical practice, and we publish the results whether they are good or bad. Every programme gets a safety assessment before it reaches patients and is monitored afterwards.

Suppliers and partners are held to the same standard. The supplier code of conduct covers working conditions, safety and the environment.

We are cutting emissions from our laboratories and offices and report on progress in the annual report.
