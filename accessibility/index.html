Accessibility statement

Last updated: 1 September 2028

NeuroStat wants everyone to be able to use this website. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, level AA.

What we have done

  • The site can be used with a keyboard and with screen readers.
  • Text and background colours meet the contrast ratios required by level AA.
  • Images have text alternatives, and charts have a text summary.
  • Text can be enlarged to 200% without loss of content, and pages adapt to small screens.

Known limitations

Some PDF documents, mainly older reports, may not be fully accessible to screen readers. If you need a document in another format, please contact us and we will provide it.

Feedback

If you find a problem, write to web@neurostat.com. We aim to reply within five working days.
