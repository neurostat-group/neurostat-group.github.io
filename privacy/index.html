Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 September 2028

1. Who is responsible

The controller for this website is NeuroStat Holding AG, Thurgauerstrasse 66, 8050 Zürich, Switzerland. We process personal data in line with the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where it applies, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). You can reach our data protection office at privacy@neurostat.com.

2. What we process

When you visit the site, our hosting provider records technical data such as your IP address, the date and time of the request and the page requested. We use it to deliver the site and keep it secure.

When you send an enquiry, subscribe to alerts or the newsletter, or fill in any other form, we process what you enter. We use it to reply or to send what you asked for.

3. Reporting side effects

If you tell us about a side effect, we are required by law to record it and to pass it on to the authorities. We keep the report for as long as the product is on the market and for the period required by law afterwards.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

This website uses essential cookies and browser storage to remember your cookie choice and, for sections intended for healthcare professionals, your confirmation. These are needed for the site to work. With your consent we would also use optional cookies to understand how the site is used. You can change your choice at any time using “Cookie settings” in the footer.

5. Recipients and transfers

We use service providers for hosting and communication. They process data on our instructions. Some of them are located outside Switzerland and the EU. Where this is the case, we rely on adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses.

6. Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, for correction or deletion, and for restriction or portability, and you can object to processing. You can also lodge a complaint with the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner or your local supervisory authority.

7. Retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected for or as the law requires.
