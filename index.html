Medicine at the edge

Medicine far from a hospital

On polar stations, desert sites, offshore installations and military bases people work long rotations in the cold and dark, a long way from the nearest hospital. Baseline and Halo let the occupational physician see how a crew is sleeping and thinking before something goes wrong. Transport, station support and security behind these placements come from our group companies Hiemal Polar Services, Eremos Desert Operations and Aegis Corporation.

Since 2027 we have worked with the crew of Ødemark A, an installation in the northern Norwegian Sea operated by Kelvara Petrochemicals AS, on how long winter rotations affect sleep and cognition.
