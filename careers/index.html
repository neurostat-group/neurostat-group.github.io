Positions in Zürich, and field positions with our partners.

Life at NeuroStat

About 1,100 of us work in Zürich, Basel, Boston and London: clinicians, neuroscientists, statisticians, engineers and regulatory specialists. We work in small groups, read each other's work openly and make a point of meeting the people who use what we build.

Benefits include flexible hours, a research sabbatical after five years and a budget for further education.

Placements with partner companies

After two years with us you can apply for a placement of three to twelve months at one of our partner companies. Current placement partners are Roche and Novartis in Basel, Lundbeck in Copenhagen, Biogen in Cambridge, Massachusetts, and AstraZeneca in Cambridge, United Kingdom. Your manager and the partner's own HR team arrange the details with you, and your employment with NeuroStat continues during the placement.

Partners also send researchers to us. Some of the people you meet in Zürich and Basel are guests from these companies.

Field positions with our partners

NeuroStat works with partner companies that offer field positions in demanding environments, including offshore work in the Norwegian Sea.

Ødemark A, Norwegian Sea — Kelvara Petrochemicals AS. Most positions for the 2028/29 winter rotation have been filled. Current openings are listed on Kelvara's careers page.

Security and logistics positions are advertised separately by Aegis Corporation, polar station positions by Hiemal Polar Services and desert site positions by Eremos Desert Operations.
