Press releases

  • Science 12 October 2028

    NeuroStat presents circadian realignment data at an international sleep research congress

    In healthy volunteers put through a simulated eight-hour shift, NS-455 advanced the melatonin rhythm, and more so at higher doses.

  • Clinical 14 September 2028

    NeuroStat begins recruitment in BOREAS-2, a Phase II study of NS-760

    The study will assess whether NS-760 helps maintain cognitive performance during extended winter rotations on offshore installations at high latitude.

  • Investors 24 August 2028

    Half-year 2028: revenue of CHF 603 million, core operating profit up 6 percent

    Lunavel and the digital products drove growth in the first half. Full-year guidance is unchanged.

  • Products 7 June 2028

    NeuroStat Halo adds fatigue-risk reporting for occupational health teams

    Occupational physicians now get a weekly summary of sleep and fatigue trends for a whole crew.

  • Corporate 20 April 2028

    Annual General Meeting of NeuroStat Holding AG approves all proposals

    Shareholders approved the annual report, a dividend of CHF 1.10 per share and the re-election of the Board.

  • Investors 23 February 2028

    Full-year 2027: revenue of CHF 1,184 million

    Revenue grew 3 percent. The Board proposes a dividend of CHF 1.10 per share.

  • Products 21 February 2028

    NeuroStat Fathom whole-body imaging system receives CE marking

    The patient floats in a tank of breathable fluid while sound waves scan the whole body in one session.

  • Clinical 16 November 2027

    NeuroStat starts a Phase I study of NS-529 for postoperative delirium

    The first participants have been dosed in a single-ascending-dose study in healthy older adults.

  • Clinical 12 March 2026

    Phase III study of tolugepant in chronic migraine prevention does not meet its primary endpoint

    The acute indication for Migrestil is not affected. The prevention programme is discontinued.
